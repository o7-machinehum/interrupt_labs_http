Give Blackhat a keyboard. Blackpants replaces the Flipper Zero as the carrier for
your [Flipper Blackhat](/flipper-blackhat/), adding a backlit QWERTY keyboard, two
extra USB-A ports, and a 480 × 480 LCD—the same screen used in Edgar Case 2.0.

Blackpants comes assembled. **Flipper Blackhat is required and sold separately.**
Follow the [installation guide](https://github.com/o7-machinehum/flipper-blackhat/wiki/Blackpants)
when connecting the boards. The [Blackpants design files](https://github.com/o7-machinehum/Blackpants)
are open source.
