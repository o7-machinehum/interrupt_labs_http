Give Blackhat a keyboard.

Blackpants replaces the Flipper Zero as the carrier for your Blackhat. It adds a backlit QWERTY keyboard, two extra USB-A ports, and a 480 × 480 LCD—the same screen used in Edgar Case 2.0.

Blackpants comes assembled. Flipper Blackhat is required and sold separately. Follow the installation guide when connecting the boards.
